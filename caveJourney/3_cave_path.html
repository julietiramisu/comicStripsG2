THE CAVE

Mufasa squeezes into a dark cave, hoping it will lead him out of the forest. As he walks deeper inside, he hears a loud rumble behind him.

The entrance collapses. Dust fills the air. When it finally clears, he sees it: a huge dog guarding the only exit.

Mufasa's heart races. He has two choices: stand his ground, or try to quietly slip past.
